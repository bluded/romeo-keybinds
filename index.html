Shared inputs

These keys trigger more than one mod with the same press. Rebind one of them in MCM if that gets in the way.

    Tap vs. hold on the same key

    These keys split actions by tap and hold. A tap-mode action fires on key-down, so holding the key can fire it as well.

    • F tap: ledge grab · 2×: inspect weapon · hold: throw glowstick (plus the game's Use)
    • H tap: exo PSU power · hold: gas mask on/off
    • ' tap: PAW quick pin · hold: quick action wheel
    • R tap: reload, which also unjams a jammed weapon · hold: ammo check

    Cinematic camera controls

    Press - to fly the camera. It takes every key until you exit.

    • WASD move
    • Ctrl + W/S up / down
    • ⇧ fast · V toggle slow · wheel: cycle speed
    • - or Esc exit
